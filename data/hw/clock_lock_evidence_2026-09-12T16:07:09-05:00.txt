Clock Lock Evidence — REPACSS H100 NVL Node rpg-93-4
=====================================================
Captured during Slurm job 154420, 2026-09-12

SUMMARY
-------
All 4 GPUs (H100 NVL, 132 SMs each, 93.1 GiB HBM) have SM clocks pinned
at 345 MHz — the minimum supported frequency. Max boost is 1785 MHz. No
throttle reasons active other than GPU_IDLE. Applications Clocks are
"deprecated" (H100 uses a different mechanism). User does not have
permission to change clocks (nvidia-smi -rgc fails with permission error).
Power limits are set to 400W (max), above the 310W default — so power is
NOT the constraint. This is a clock lock, not a power cap.
