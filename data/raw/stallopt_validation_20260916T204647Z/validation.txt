  Paper values are MEASURED on their CXL prototype; our values are
  pure model predictions.  Key findings:
    - BW-prop is clearly worst in both workloads:  CONFIRMED.
    - Cal.Stall-opt beats Equal:  CONFIRMED in both workloads.
    - Stall-opt vs Cal.Stall-opt ordering differs from paper:
      Model finds Stall-opt best (it IS the mathematical optimum for
      the idealized pipeline).  The paper's measured results show
      Cal.Stall-opt winning because the delta correction compensates
      for real system overheads (protocol latency, scheduling jitter)
      that the model does not capture.  This is expected behavior,
      not a bug -- it is exactly WHY calibration exists.
