2c. METASTABILITY PROBE — does the queue outlive the episode?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Constant BW with ONE injected 60s degradation (depth 2.3), then full
    bandwidth recovery at t=360s. Drain time = how long after recovery N
    takes to return to its pre-episode level. Persistence = drain / 60s.
    load 70%: preN=2.72 duringN=20.00 peakN=42  -> queue OUTLIVES its 60s cause: drains in 94s (1.57x the episode)
    load 85%: preN=10.78 duringN=46.85 peakN=68  -> queue OUTLIVES its 60s cause: drains in 155s (2.58x the episode)
    load 95%: preN=28.53 duringN=61.48 peakN=76  -> INTRINSIC INSTABILITY — N already climbing pre-episode (N 22.0->35.1); load exceeds effective capacity, not episode-induced
